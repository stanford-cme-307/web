Use the primal LP min⁡{c⊺x:Ax=b,x≥0}\min\{c^\intercal x:Ax=b,\ x\ge0\} and its dual max⁡{b⊺y:A⊺y+s=c,s≥0}\max\{b^\intercal y:A^\intercal y+s=c,\ s\ge0\}. Write X=diag⁡(x)X=\operatorname{diag}(x) and S=diag⁡(s)S=\operatorname{diag}(s). In the Newton systems, QQ is the symmetric quadratic objective matrix, which is zero for an LP.

1. Complementarity and duality gap

Show how the complementarity residual x⊺sx^\intercal s controls the duality gap when xx and (y,s)(y,s) are primal and dual feasible, respectively.

Solution to exercise 1

Dual feasibility gives c=A⊺y+sc=A^\intercal y+s. Therefore c⊺x−b⊺y=y⊺(Ax−b)+s⊺x=x⊺sc^\intercal x-b^\intercal y=y^\intercal(Ax-b)+s^\intercal x=x^\intercal s by primal feasibility.

Solution to exercise 2

It is x⊺sx^\intercal s by Exercise 1 and lies in [0,ε][0,\varepsilon]. Weak duality then places the optimal value between b⊺yb^\intercal y and c⊺xc^\intercal x.

4. Feasibility of a full Newton step

Solution to exercise 4

For example, take Q=0Q=0, A=[11]A=[1\ 1], x=(1,1)x=(1,1), s=(1,4)s=(1,4), and μ=0.1\mu=0.1, with b=2b=2, c=sc=s, and y=0y=0. 5. The augmented Newton system

Consider AΔx=rp,−QΔx+A⊺Δy+Δs=rd,SΔx+XΔs=rc, \begin{aligned} A\Delta x&=r_p,\\ -Q\Delta x+A^\intercal\Delta y+\Delta s&=r_d,\\ S\Delta x+X\Delta s&=r_c, \end{aligned} with rp=b−Axr_p=b-Ax, rd=c−A⊺y−s+Qxr_d=c-A^\intercal y-s+Qx, and rc=μ𝟏−XS𝟏r_c=\mu\mathbf 1-XS\mathbf 1. Eliminate Δs\Delta s. What are the advantages and disadvantages of solving the augmented system?

Solution to exercise 5

Since XX is invertible, Δs=X−1(rc−SΔx)\Delta s=X^{-1}(r_c-S\Delta x). Substituting gives the augmented equations −(Q+X−1S)Δx+A⊺Δy=rd−X−1rc,AΔx=rp. \begin{aligned} -(Q+X^{-1}S)\Delta x+A^\intercal\Delta y&=r_d-X^{-1}r_c,\\ A\Delta x&=r_p. \end{aligned} The system is smaller and symmetric, and Δs\Delta s is recovered with a diagonal solve. The augmented matrix is generally indefinite, which can require pivoting, and widely varying entries of X−1SX^{-1}S can cause scaling difficulties near the solution.

Solution to exercise 7

The LP KKT conditions are primal feasibility, dual feasibility, and xisi=0x_i s_i=0 for each ii. The central path replaces complementary products by xisi=μx_i s_i=\mu. Feasibility gives c⊺x−b⊺y=x⊺sc^\intercal x-b^\intercal y=x^\intercal s, and summing the nn complementary products gives nμn\mu.
