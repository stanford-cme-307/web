1. Subgradients of the Euclidean norm at zero

For f(x)=∥x∥2f(x)=\|x\|_2, compute ∂f(0)\partial f(0) using directional derivatives or limits of gradients.

Solution to exercise 1

For a direction dd, f′(0;d)=∥d∥2f'(0;d)=\|d\|_2. The subgradient condition is g⊺d≤∥d∥2g^\intercal d\le\|d\|_2 for every dd. This holds if ∥g∥2≤1\|g\|_2\le1 by Cauchy–Schwarz, and taking d=gd=g proves necessity. So ∂f(0)={g:∥g∥2≤1}\partial f(0)=\{g:\|g\|_2\le1\}.

2. An eigenvalue with multiplicity two

For symmetric XX, ∂λmax(X)=conv⁡{vv⊺:∥v∥2=1,Xv=λmax(X)v}\partial\lambda_{\max}(X)=\operatorname{conv}\{vv^\intercal:\|v\|_2=1,\ Xv=\lambda_{\max}(X)v\}. Compute the set for X=[200020001].X=\begin{bmatrix}2&0&0\\0&2&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}.

Solution to exercise 2

The top eigenspace is span⁡{e1,e2}\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_2\}. The convex hull of the unit rank-one projectors on that space consists of positive semidefinite matrices supported on the first two coordinates with trace 1. This follows by spectral decomposition. Explicitly the set consists of [ab0b1−a0000],0≤a≤1,b2≤a(1−a).\begin{bmatrix}a&b&0\\b&1-a&0\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}, \qquad 0\le a\le1,\quad b^2\le a(1-a).

3. A maximum of three affine functions

Let f(x)=max⁡{x1+2x2,3x1−x2,−x1+3x2}f(x)=\max\{x_1+2x_2,3x_1-x_2,-x_1+3x_2\}. At x=(1,1)x=(1,1), identify the active terms and find the full subdifferential.

Solution to exercise 3

The values are 3,2,23,2,2 in the order shown, so only the first term is active. The subdifferential is the singleton {(1,2)}\{(1,2)\}.

4. Conjugate of a half-line indicator

Let f(x)=δ[0,∞)(x)f(x)=\delta_{[0,\infty)}(x), which is zero on [0,∞)[0,\infty) and +∞+\infty elsewhere. Compute f⋆(y)=sup⁡x≥0xyf^\star(y)=\sup_{x\ge0}xy. Use g∈∂f(x)g\in\partial f(x) if and only if f(x)+f⋆(g)=xgf(x)+f^\star(g)=xg to determine ∂f(0)\partial f(0).

Solution to exercise 4

For y≤0y\le0, the supremum is zero, attained at x=0x=0. For y>0y>0, it is +∞+\infty. At zero the Fenchel equality requires f⋆(g)=0f^\star(g)=0, so ∂f(0)=(−∞,0]\partial f(0)=(-\infty,0].

5. A subgradient step at and away from a kink

Let f(x)=|x|+5f(x)=|x|+5 and take one subgradient step with step size α=0.4\alpha=0.4 from x0=3x_0=3. Separately, list all possible next iterates for a step started at x0=0x_0=0.

Solution to exercise 5

At 33 the sole subgradient is 11, so x1=3−0.4=2.6x_1=3-0.4=2.6. At 00 the subdifferential is [−1,1][-1,1]. The next iterate is −0.4g-0.4g for any gg in that interval, giving [−0.4,0.4][-0.4,0.4].

6. Projection onto a box

Let C={x∈ℝ2:0≤xi≤1,i=1,2}C=\{x\in\mathbb R^2:0\le x_i\le1,\ i=1,2\}. The proximal operator of its indicator is the Euclidean projection onto CC. Compute it at w=(3,4)w=(3,4).

Solution to exercise 6

The squared-distance objective is separable, so project each coordinate onto [0,1][0,1]. Both coordinates exceed the upper bound, giving (1,1)(1,1).

7. A proximal step with a constraint

For f(x)=12(x−5)2+δ[0,∞)(x)f(x)=\tfrac12(x-5)^2+\delta_{[0,\infty)}(x), find prox⁡tf(v)\operatorname{prox}_{tf}(v) at v=1v=1 and t=1t=1.

Solution to exercise 7

Minimize 12(x−5)2+12(x−1)2\tfrac12(x-5)^2+\tfrac12(x-1)^2 over x≥0x\ge0. The derivative 2x−62x-6 vanishes at x=3x=3, which is feasible and uniquely minimizes the strictly convex objective.

8. Fixed points of a proximal operator

Let f(x)=|x−2|f(x)=|x-2| and T(x)=prox⁡f(x)T(x)=\operatorname{prox}_f(x). Find all fixed points of TT and explain their relation to 0∈∂f(x)0\in\partial f(x).

Solution to exercise 8

The point xx is fixed exactly when it minimizes f(z)+12(z−x)2f(z)+\tfrac12(z-x)^2 over zz. At z=xz=x the quadratic has zero derivative, so this is equivalent to 0∈∂f(x)0\in\partial f(x). The only minimizer of |x−2||x-2| is 22, giving the sole fixed point.
